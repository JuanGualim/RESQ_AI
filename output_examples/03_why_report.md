## Situation
- Segment T6 is blocked by the coordinator, preventing routes through that segment.

## Plan
- Resources are prioritized by risk score, with highest risk (0.81) communities C07 and C04 receiving full allocation first via vehicle V2 or V4.
- Communities are served in tiers: C07 (priority 1-3), C12 (priority 4-6), C03 (priority 7-9), and C05 (priority 10-12) receive water, food, and medical kits via routes avoiding the blocked T6 segment.
- Vehicle assignments shifted from V1/T3-T6 routes to V2/T3-T7-T9 or V4/T1-T3-T7 routes to adapt to the blocked segment and vehicle availability.
- Unserved communities C01, C08, C11 have no resources assigned due to stock exhaustion and route constraints.

## Why the plan changed
The plan changed because segment T6 was blocked by the coordinator, making routes through T6 infeasible. This event forced re-routing and resource reallocation, resulting in the specific changes listed in changes_vs_previous, such as removal of blankets and reductions in food and water quantities for several communities as vehicles were redirected to alternative routes.
